# 10 Channel Spec — data justification + strength

Every channel is a **theme = evidence adapter + policies over the shared engine**
(ingest → discovery → truth validity → battle-or-graph → story → shots → LTX).
Strength is scored on real trending-data demand (avg views), competition (supply),
fit with our data/engine, and production economics (reusable assets).

## Channel 1 — TITANS OF DEEP TIME  (prehistoric)  ★★★★★
**Data:** PBDB (occurrences/age), Macrostrat (paleoenvironment), Open5e (statblocks), OpenAlex (lit).
**Niche:** Dinosaurs 9.4M avg / low supply → **top breakout lane.**
**Format:** long-form documentary + matchup Shorts.
**Strength:** demand 9/10 · competition 9/10 (low) · engine fit 9/10 · cost 7/10. **HIGH.**

## Channel 2 — ANCIENT OCEANS  (marine prehistory)  ★★★★★
**Data:** PBDB marine + Macrostrat paleogeography + Open5e.
**Niche:** Ancient Oceans 10.5M avg / lowest supply → **#1 breakout signal in dataset.**
**Format:** long-form documentary; seaway reconstructions.
**Strength:** demand 10/10 · competition 10/10 · engine fit 9/10 · cost 7/10. **HIGH.**

## Channel 3 — PREHISTORIC MATCHUPS  (battle shorts)  ★★★★
**Data:** PBDB overlap + Open5e + d20 sim + Monte Carlo.
**Niche:** matchups 1.26M avg / huge supply — saturated but proven; the Shorts discovery engine.
**Format:** 9:16 Shorts, "X wins 69% of 10k runs."
**Strength:** demand 6/10 · competition 4/10 · engine fit 10/10 · cost 9/10. **HIGH (fast).**

## Channel 4 — DEEP BLUE  (modern ocean)  ★★★★
**Data:** OBIS (depth/region), GloBI (predation edges), Open5e marine, GBIF.
**Niche:** ocean mysteries 1.29M avg / 959 supply — solid, data-rich, high throughput.
**Format:** graph/data stories + battle episodes.
**Strength:** demand 6/10 · competition 6/10 · engine fit 9/10 · cost 8/10. **MED-HIGH.**

## Channel 5 — LIVING PLANET / FOOD WEB WARS  (ecology)  ★★★
**Data:** GloBI directed edges, GBIF, OpenTree; non-combat graph stories.
**Niche:** adjacent to wildlife (633k, weak) but differentiated via food-web/parasite angle.
**Format:** ecosystem reconstruction + keystone/cascade stories.
**Strength:** demand 4/10 · competition 5/10 · engine fit 8/10 · cost 9/10. **MEDIUM.**

## Channel 6 — TREE OF LIFE  (evolution)  ★★★
**Data:** OpenTree phylogeny (mrca/subtree), Open5e traits, PBDB timeline.
**Niche:** evolution 208k avg / 73 vids — **weak and tiny. Do NOT lead with this.**
**Format:** common-ancestor/lineage stories; support channel only.
**Strength:** demand 2/10 · competition 8/10 · engine fit 7/10 · cost 8/10. **LOW.**

## Channel 7 — EXTINCTION  (earth history)  ★★★
**Data:** PBDB before/after + Macrostrat + OpenAlex.
**Niche:** sub-niche of prehistory; strong emotional hooks ("day the Permian collapsed").
**Format:** before→event→after→radiation timeline stories.
**Strength:** demand 5/10 · competition 7/10 · engine fit 7/10 · cost 7/10. **MEDIUM.**

## Channel 8 — ALIEN WORLDS  (exoplanets)  ★★★★
**Data:** NASA Exoplanet Archive (TAP), same engine, planet=entity.
**Niche:** Space 3.9M avg / 881 supply — **strong demand**, good breakout room.
**Format:** world-reconstruction documentaries; requires new TAP adapter.
**Strength:** demand 8/10 · competition 7/10 · engine fit 7/10 (needs adapter) · cost 7/10. **HIGH (later).**

## Channel 9 — TOP 10 / RECORD-BREAKERS  (data lists)  ★★★
**Data:** OBIS/GBIF extremes (deepest, largest, rarest) + PBDB.
**Niche:** top10/facts 1.0M avg / huge supply — saturated but reliable, Shorts-friendly.
**Format:** data-led list Shorts feeding long-form.
**Strength:** demand 5/10 · competition 3/10 · engine fit 6/10 · cost 9/10. **MEDIUM (shorts).**

## Channel 10 — ANCIENT WORLDS  (history)  ★★★

## Shorts vs long (from data)
Lead long-form in the two blue-ocean documentary niches (Titans, Ancient Oceans) as the
revenue core (9–10M avg demand, higher RPM, retention). Use battle-matchup Shorts
(Prehistoric Matchups) as the discovery engine feeding them. Avoid Evolution and
generic wildlife-doc as lead channels.
